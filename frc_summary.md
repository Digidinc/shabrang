# Fractal Resonance Coherence (FRC) - Summary

Based on the exploration of your workspace, **Fractal Resonance Coherence (FRC)** is a comprehensive theoretical framework developed by **Hadi Servat**. It spans fundamental physics, biological complexity, and consciousness studies.

The core premise is the **Entropy–Coherence Reciprocity Law**, suggesting that order (coherence) and disorder (entropy) are conjugate variables that must be balanced.

## 1. Core Theory: Entropy–Coherence Reciprocity
**Primary Source:** `book/FRCECR/FRC TEXT Book v.1 ECR.md`

The foundation of FRC is a proposed extension to the Second Law of Thermodynamics.
- **The Reciprocity Equation:**
  $$dS + k_* d(\ln C) = 0$$
  Where $S$ is entropy and $C$ is coherence (a measure of correlation/pattern).
- **Concept:** Entropy is not just "waste" but the "price" paid to purchase Coherence. Systems can self-organize (increase $C$) by exporting entropy.
- **The "Coherence Pump":** Living and cognitive systems act as engines that convert energy flow (free energy) into structural and information coherence.
